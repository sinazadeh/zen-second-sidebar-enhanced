# Composition brief

Create a 1920x1080 Hyperframes composition for Zen Second Sidebar Enhanced. It should feel like a polished native-browser feature reveal rather than a generic SaaS ad.

Use the current settings-page palette: background `#18171d`, cards `#222129`, raised surfaces `#2a2932`, text `#ecebf2`, muted text `#9a98a8`, accent `#8f82ff`. Use system UI typography, generous rounding, thin white borders, and controlled violet glow.

Build five continuous scenes on one seekable GSAP timeline. Put real browser footage in the opening frame and keep product evidence central throughout. Use the current Playwright settings-page capture instead of recreating its controls. Do not use letter tiles that could imply official AI integrations, and do not invent product capabilities or control types.

Time key reveals to 3.40, 8.20, 12.20, and 15.30 seconds. Keep all important text in a 10% safe margin. Make the outro readable for at least 3 seconds and retain real browser UI in the poster frame. Add music across the film at low volume and use only a few isolated low-risk interface sounds.
